# Asset provenance


PixelFX by Patch: https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx (MIT; license/notice in assets/pixelfx). SUIT font license in assets/fonts/OFL.txt. Generated sprites, original generation sheets, normalization and prompts documented in assets/generated.

Enemy references: https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/ja/news/detail/162159 ; https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/ja/news/detail/163454 ; https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/159349 ; https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/126992 ; https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/112690 ; https://blog.playstation.com/2021/10/04/genshin-impact-version-2-2-dispel-the-mystic-fog-shrouding-tsurumi-island/ . Card rules, stress, injury and all numeric mechanics are original fan adaptation, not official turn-based rules.

Breacher Primus and Rifthound are distinct families. All generated sprites are fan reinterpretation. No unverified Japanese dialogue is presented as canonical.

Version 0.3: combat, card dissolution, breakdown/recovery and healing effects are original Canvas2D path drawings in `dist/vfx.js`. No external effect atlas is fetched or played at runtime. PixelFX files remain as retained source resources with their original notices; the earlier version's dependency is historical.
